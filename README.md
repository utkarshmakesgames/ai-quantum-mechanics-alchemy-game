# Quantum Forge

**Forge the universe, one particle at a time.**

Quantum Forge is a browser-based alchemy game where you combine fundamental particles and elements to discover new materials, guided by real quantum mechanics concepts.

## How to Play

1. Open the game in your browser
2. Select two elements from your inventory
3. Combine them in the forge to discover new particles and materials
4. Complete challenges and unlock journal entries to learn the science behind each discovery

## Tech Stack

- Vanilla HTML/CSS/JavaScript
- Canvas shader background
- No build step — runs directly in the browser
